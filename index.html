Driftline Studios Uploader

Our internal publishing tool. It uploads videos we produce to the channels we own, using YouTube API Services. It is a private, single-operator tool: it is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and accesses no viewer or audience data. It requests one permission scope, youtube.upload, which permits uploading a video and setting its thumbnail and nothing else.

How it handles data is set out in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

YouTube API Services

Driftline Studios Uploader uses YouTube API Services. Use of it is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Access granted to the application can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

Production and AI disclosure

Our videos use synthesised narration and are assembled with automated tooling. Editorial decisions, research, and review are made by a person. Where a video's content requires disclosure of synthetic media under YouTube's policies, that disclosure is applied.
